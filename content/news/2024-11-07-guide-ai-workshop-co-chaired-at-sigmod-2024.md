Brit Youngmann co-chaired the First Workshop on Governance, Understanding, and Integration of Data for Effective and Responsible AI ([GUIDE-AI](https://guide-ai-workshop.github.io/)) alongside Abolfazl Asudeh, Sainyam Galhorta, Amir Gilad, and Babak Salimi. The workshop was held in conjunction with SIGMOD 2024.
